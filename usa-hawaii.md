**Oahu**

Waikiki

Take a surfing lesson on Waikiki Beach.

See Hawaii’s state fish, the humuhumunukunukuapuaa, up close at the Waikiki Aquarium.

Take a picture next to the Duke Kahanamoku Statue.

Honolulu

Take a guided tour of Iolani Palace, the only Royal Palace on U.S. soil, then cross the street to view the King Kamehameha I statue.

Soak in Honolulu harbor from atop historic Aloha Tower.

Windward Coast

Drive around Makapuu Point to discover jaw-dropping views of the Pacific.

North Shore

Cool down with a “shave ice” in the laid-back surf town of Haleiwa.

See seven authentically recreated South Pacific islands at the Polynesian Cultural Center.

Watch pro surfers charge 20 to 30 foot winter waves at Waimea Bay, Banzai Pipeline, and Sunset Beach.

Central Oahu

Sample a fresh cut, sweet pineapple and get lost in Dole Plantation’s 1.7 mile garden maze.

Spend a day exploring Pearl Harbor, one of the nation’s most significant historical landmarks. View the moving exhibits at the USS Arizona Memorial and explore the Battleship Missouri Memorial, the USS Bowfin Submarine Museum and Park, and the new Pacific Aviation Museum.

Leeward Coast

Surf the wave pool at the 29 acre Hawaiian Waters Adventure Park.

Hike to the dramatic shorelines of sacred Kaena Point.

** **

**Maui**

West Maui

Snorkel at Kapalua Beach and look for humuhumunukunukuapuaa.

Stroll through historic Lahaina Town and marvel at the U.S.’s largest Banyan Tree.

Watch the surfers rip up the big waves in Honolua Bay.

Central Maui

Go on a whale watching boat tour off the coast of Kaanapali.

South Maui

Spend an afternoon at Makena and watch the sunset at Big Beach.

Have dinner in Kihei and spend the rest of the evening dancing it off.

East Maui

Plan a day to drive the narrow, windy road to Hana making frequent stops to enjoy the stunning dual views of ocean and mountains.

Stop at Waianapanapa for black sand beaches and underground pools.

Continue past Hana to Oheo Gulch to see beautiful waterfall-fed pools.

South Maui

Revel in the magnificent panoramic vistas from Ulupalakua Ranch.

Buy fresh flowers or try sweet Kula onions from the local farmers.

** **

**Big Island**

Hilo Side (East Side)

Stroll through the charming streets of historic, downtown Hilo.

Hamakua Coast

Hike through the timeless beauty of Waipio Valley, “The Valley of Kings.”

Take a short hike to see the 442-foot grandeur of Akaka Falls.

South Side

Explore the 500-square-mile Hawaii Volcanoes National Park.

Stand at the southern most point in the U.S. at Ka Lae, South Point.

Walk a black sand beach in Punaluu Beach Park.

** **

**Kauai**

North Kauai

Visit a taro farm in Hanalei and try a taste of poi, pounded from taro.

Hike the Napali Coast for unparalleled island views.

East Kauai (Coconut coast)

Explore old Kapaa Town and delight in the quaint shops and restaurants.

Enjoy a picnic at Lydgate Park and watch keiki (children) play in the sun.

Take a boat ride to the fern-draped splendor of the Fern Grotto.

Walk respectfully around ancient heiau (religious temples) in Wailua or kayak down the Wailua River, one of the only navigable rivers in Hawaii.

Lihue

It provides ample opportunity for shopping, sightseeing, and meeting the locals.

Discover Kauai's plantation past in Kilohana.

Spend a day swimming and sunbathing at Kalapaki Beach.

Drive 5 miles from Lihue to look at Wailua Falls, an 80-foot tiered waterfall.

South Kauai

See why Poipu Beach was recently voted “America’s Best Beach” by The Travel Channel.

Take a historical walking tour on the 10-mile Koloa Heritage Trail.

View the legendary Spouting Horn at sunset.

West Kauai

Head to Kekaha Beach Park and enjoy imposing views of Niihau Island.

Drive up to the awesome Waimea Canyon, the “Grand Canyon of the Pacific”.

**Molokai**

Kaunakakai

Take a stroll down Kaunakakai Wharf eating some sweet Molokai bread from Kanemitsu Bakery.

Check out Church Row and the stone foundation of the oceanfront Kamehameha V house.

Have a picnic under the palm trees at One Alii Beach Park.

West End

Soak up the sun on Papohaku Beach, one of the largest beaches in Hawaii.

East Molokai

Snorkel at Kumimi Beach at mile marker 20.

Take a guided tour into Halawa Valley, learn about its history, and see the 250-foot Mooula Falls.

Take a mule ride in Kalaupapa National Historic Park and learn about the works of Father Damien.

**Lanai**

Lanai City

Experience local food at its best at The Blue Ginger.

Relax at the Hotel Lanai or The Lodge at Koele.

Have a picnic under the pines at Dole Park.

Manele Bay

Take a boat out to see Puu Pehe or Sweetheart Rock.

Golf on a cliffside at the spectacular Challenge at Manele.

Snorkel and swim at Hulopoe Beach Park.
